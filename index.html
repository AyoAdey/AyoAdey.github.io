About me

I'm Ayomide, a data analyst who asks "is this number right?" before "what does it mean?"

I studied Economics at Babcock University. My final-year project used regression analysis to measure how information technology affects Nigeria's economic growth, and it's where I fell for working with data.

After graduating I spent almost two years running administration at a school in Kaduna. I coordinated the academic, finance and admin teams, tracked budgets across four departments and kept the records accurate. It taught me how much a business depends on data people can trust.

Today I'm a Data Analyst Intern at Wema Bank, working on data quality, SQL and Power BI reporting. I've completed NYSC, and I'm looking for a graduate trainee or data analyst role where careful analysis leads to real decisions.
